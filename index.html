How It Works

The loop is simple — memorize, recreate, score

Each round follows the same five-step sequence. The game stays simple so your color memory is what gets tested — not your ability to navigate menus.

01

Memorize the color

A swatch appears on screen. You have a few seconds to study it. Every detail matters — hue family, saturation intensity, brightness level. Your brain will forget most of it within seconds.

02

Swatch disappears

The color is removed from view. Now you're working from visual memory alone. You have to reconstruct what you just saw using nothing but your recollection.

03

Recreate using HSB sliders

Use the Hue (0-360°), Saturation (0-100%), and Brightness (0-100%) sliders to recreate the color. Fine-tune each channel until it looks right. Then lock in your guess.

04

Score out of 10

Each round is scored 0-10 using CIELAB perceptual distance. The closer your HSB match is to the original in perceptual space, the higher your score.

05

Compare and share

Five rounds, max 50. Post your score, challenge a friend to beat it, or jump into the daily challenge where everyone competes on the same set of colors.

Deep Dive

What Is Dialed GG? How The Color Memory Game Works

A clear explanation of what Dialed GG is, who made it, how the scoring works, and how to get started.

What exactly is Dialed GG?

Dialed.gg (also written as dialedgg, dialled gg, or dialed. gg) is a free online color memory game that challenges how well humans can actually recall colors. The premise is simple: you're shown five colors one at a time, given a brief moment to memorize each one, then asked to recreate it from memory using Hue, Saturation, and Brightness sliders. Your score is calculated using perceptual color science.

Unlike other color games, Dialed GG doesn't use multiple choice. You can't guess your way to a good score. You have to genuinely remember the color and reconstruct it on an HSB field. That distinction is what makes the game both frustrating and addictive.

How is scoring calculated?

Each color is converted to the CIELAB perceptual color space. The distance between your guess and the original is measured using CIEDE2000, which accounts for how human vision actually perceives color differences. Getting the right hue family earns bonus points, while wrong hues on vivid colors incur a larger penalty.

Maximum score per round is 10, for a total of 50 across five rounds. Most players score between 4-8 per round on their first attempt. High scores in the 8-9 range per round are exceptional.

What game modes are available?

There are three core modes: Solo — play at your own pace, practice, and track your personal best. Daily — one shot at five global colors that everyone on Earth gets. Multiplayer — host a live round or share a challenge link for asynchronous play. All modes support Easy difficulty (HSB hints), Hard mode (no hints after the first round), and Brutal mode (every color is shown once before any can be recreated).
